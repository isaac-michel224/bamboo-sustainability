## Overview
The goal is to provide information to countries in the Caribbean and Latin America ways they can invest in using sustainable material for construction projects. 



## Bambusa Vulgaris

Also known as 'common bamboo', the plant is mostly found throughout tropical part of South Asia.  

B. Vulgaris has various utilities. It can be used as construction material in building shelters and create flooring for the house.  The plant can be used in making boat materials, furniture, and for food as well.   

Another major quality is that it prevents soil erosion, and helps keep it in place. 



We were able to pinpoint some key factors for bamboo to thrive in Latin America & the Caribbean.  This can help pinpoint some places where B. Vulgaris can grow productively.
